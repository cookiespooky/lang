## Target language

French (`fr`).

## Primary goal

Recover and develop practical conversational French for natural communication with French-speaking acquaintances. The priority is being able to speak and understand in real interaction rather than passing an exam or completing a textbook syllabus.

## Language-learning background

- Studied French for approximately one year at a linguistics faculty about 15 years ago.
- French was a non-major/secondary language studied mainly to satisfy course requirements, so the original proficiency was limited and much has since become dormant.
- Speaks English fluently.
- Can communicate in German.
- Has a linguistics background, so explicit linguistic comparison/terminology can be used when it genuinely accelerates learning, but the goal remains active conversational French rather than analysis of French.

Preliminary only; refine through actual French use rather than restarting from zero.

> Je parle anglais et allemand. J’invente web services

This shows recoverable basic sentence-building ability and vocabulary, alongside substantial gaps in morphology, articles/prepositions, idiomatic lexical choice and orthography. See `state.md` for the working assessment.

## Stable learner facts relevant to teaching

- Prior exposure means some French knowledge may be dormant rather than genuinely new; attempt retrieval before assuming a concept needs full first-time instruction.
- English and German can provide useful contrastive anchors when appropriate.
- Conversational usefulness is more important than exhaustive grammatical sequencing.
