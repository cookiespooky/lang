# First conversational reactivation — 2026-08-16

## Practiced / observed

Short spontaneous conversation about yesterday, tomorrow, work and a prospective client.

Learner production included:

> J’ai ecri code hier. Demain je vas planner ma semain

> Je vais travailler avec ma customers potential et je vais parler avec ils avec telefon. Nous venons discuter notre project

> C’est une companie de ecologie. Je vais leur proposer faire le web service.

## Evidence of reactivation

- Spontaneously attempted passé composé to describe yesterday (`J’ai ecri...`).
- Spontaneously attempted near future for tomorrow; after correction, `je vais` was subsequently produced correctly.
- After `leur parler` was introduced in feedback, learner independently reused `leur` in the next turn: `Je vais leur proposer...`.
- Can maintain communicative intent across multiple turns despite substantial lexical/morphological gaps.

## Current friction

- Frequent English transfer when active French vocabulary is missing (`planner`, `customers potential`, `project`, `web service`).
- Verb complementation not yet reliable (`proposer faire` instead of `proposer de faire`).
- Gender/articles/agreement and orthography remain unstable.
- French lexical chunks for professional/business conversation need reconstruction.

## Change from baseline

The initial baseline only demonstrated simple present-tense self-description. This interaction establishes that the learner can also spontaneously reach for past and future reference and can rapidly reuse a corrected pronoun structure in a new sentence. The active system is fragmentary but more generative than the first sample alone suggested.

## Next

Continue contextual conversation. Recycle `leur`, `aller + infinitif`, and introduce/recover `proposer de + infinitif` naturally. Probe reasons, requirements and project description while replacing recurring English transfers with high-value French chunks.

Assessment confidence: moderate for the observed patterns; broader tense/pronoun competence remains untested.
