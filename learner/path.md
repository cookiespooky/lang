# Learning path

Status: active — provisional
Last updated: 2026-08-16

This is a near-term working hypothesis. Revise it aggressively when direct evidence suggests a better route.

## Current objective

Reactivate dormant French and establish a reliable conversational core rather than restarting with a generic A1 syllabus.

## Immediate priorities

1. **Validate the baseline through conversation**
   - Expand beyond self-introduction.
   - Test comprehension and spontaneous response.
   - Probe present, past and future reference naturally.
   - Identify what is dormant vs. genuinely absent.

2. **Rebuild high-frequency conversational sentence production**
   - self, work, interests, everyday activities, people, places;
   - common verbs and their usable forms;
   - negation and high-frequency questions;
   - articles/prepositions where errors actually appear.

3. **Reduce English/Russian lexical transfer in active production**
   - prefer natural French lexical chunks over word-for-word construction;
   - collect only recurring/high-value examples.

4. **Move recovered knowledge into spontaneous use**
   - short contextual exchanges;
   - progressively less prompting;
   - recycle recently recovered structures in later conversation.

## Do not do yet

- Do not march through a complete A1 grammar syllabus by default.
- Do not assign a precise CEFR level before enough direct evidence exists.
- Do not front-load long grammar explanations when retrieval/testing can reveal what the learner already knows.

## Next interaction

Begin primarily in accessible French. Use a natural conversation to test and reactivate the existing system. Correct selectively, record meaningful evidence during the interaction, and update this path as soon as stronger priorities emerge.
